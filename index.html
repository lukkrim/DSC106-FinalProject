Listening guide

What to listen for

01

Rhythm

Notice whether the beat feels danceable, relaxed, syncopated, or driven by rap flow.

02

Energy

Listen for intensity: bright synths, heavy drums, distorted guitars, or smoother production.

03

Voice

Pay attention to vocal hooks, rap verses, vocal chops, harmonies, or mostly instrumental moments.

04

Texture

Compare acoustic, electronic, polished, gritty, spacious, and club-like sounds before choosing.

    What we learned

    Three things the data taught us

    01

    Genre has a real sound

    Each genre clusters around distinct audio traits — speechiness, energy, acousticness. Your ears weren't wrong; the data agrees.

    02

    Sound alone doesn't make a hit

    Audio traits explain surprisingly little of a song's popularity. Timing, artist recognition, and cultural moment matter far more than any fingerprint.

    03

    Music shifts slowly — then all at once

    Year by year, genres drift. Some traits converge across the whole landscape. Others diverge sharply. The changes are subtle until suddenly they aren't.
